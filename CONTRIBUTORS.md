# Contributors

SikBodo is improved by the people who use it. This file lists everyone who has
contributed, and what they contributed.

**How to be listed:** make a contribution — a correction, a word, a phrase, a bug report, a
code change — through any of the routes described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) or on the
[Contribute page](https://apsideslabs.github.io/SikBodo/contribute.html). You will be added
here when your change is merged. If you would rather not be named, say so and you will not be.

## What counts as a contribution

You do not have to write code, and you do not need a GitHub account. All of the following are
recorded here when they are merged:

| Contribution | Typical effort |
|---|---|
| Reporting a wrong word, phrase or translation | 5 minutes |
| Adding a dialect note for your region | 5 minutes |
| Reporting a bug, a typo or a broken link | 2 minutes |
| Adding a word to the dictionary | 10 minutes |
| Adding a phrase or a dialogue | 15 minutes |
| Improving an explanation that confused you | 10 minutes |
| Improving accessibility, performance or layout | varies |
| Translating the interface | varies |
| Improving the documentation | 15 minutes |
